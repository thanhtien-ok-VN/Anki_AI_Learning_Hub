- secret_word：适合该级别的秘密词语
- forbidden_words：4-5 个相关词语，描述秘密词语时不能使用这些词
- ai_description：不使用任何禁忌词来描述秘密词语

{{ "rounds": [{{ "secret_word": "...", "forbidden_words": ["A","B","C","D"], "ai_description": "...", "category": "...", "difficulty": "medium" }}] }}
